# Vision

A recruiter-facing portfolio that communicates David’s strongest evidence in
seconds, then rewards curiosity with kinetic detail.

## Product principles

- Proof before biography: Mastercard, CERN ATLAS, and T23 lead the story.
- Motion carries narrative: the entrance assembles information in priority order.
- The page remains understandable without interaction or animation.
- Copy is short, specific, and repeatable after a five-second scan.
- Visual language comes from signal detection, systems, and instrumentation—not
  generic portfolio cards.

## MVP direction: Signal Room

- Industrial single-typeface system built from the bundled SF Mono family.
- Warm technical canvas with one active signal color at a time.
- Character-state headline reveal adapted from `prompts.txt`.
- Adjacent color tiles assemble and reshuffle as a secondary motion layer.
- Evidence selector changes the active proof and palette.
- Project rows provide a compact second layer for interested interviewers.

The WebGL mask reveal in `prompts.txt` is intentionally parked until the core
information hierarchy and motion rhythm are validated.
