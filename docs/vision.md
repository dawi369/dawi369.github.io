# Artistic Vision: The White Gallery

**Core Aesthetic:** Clean. Silent. Bright.

This website is a digital art gallery. It does not shout; it presents. The background is stark white (or a very subtle off-white/paper texture), creating a vast sense of space. Text is sparse, used only as a label or a quiet guide. The content—images and code—hangs on the "walls" with deliberate focus.

## Aesthetic Principles

- **Light & Space:** A "Light Mode" first experience. Maximized whitespace prevents overcrowding.
- **The Gallery Feel:** Content is treated as distinct exhibits.
- **Typography:** Minimalist sans-serif (e.g., Inter, Helvetica, or a monospaced font for code elements). Small, neat, purposeful.
- **Interaction:** Smooth, intuitive flow. No jarring animations, just simple transitions that feel like walking from one painting to another.

## Reference Style

Think of a modern art gallery in Berlin or Tokyo.

- White walls.
- Lots of space between artworks.
- Small, precise placards explaining the work.
- The art speaks for itself.

## Visual Direction

- **Images:** High quality, potentially abstract or surreal. They should "pop" against the white background.
- **Colors:** Dominant White (#ffffff, #fdfdfd). Accent Black (#171717) for text. Perhaps one subtle accent color for interactions.
